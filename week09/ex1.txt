After comparing the values obtained, I concluded that aging and nfu are more effective algorithms in general case because they take into account the time the pages have been unused. A random algorithm can be effective, but not always, it depends on the reference string. Therefore, nfu and aging are approximately the same and are effective for a large number of queries, while random sometimes gives good statistics and sometimes bad statistics.
