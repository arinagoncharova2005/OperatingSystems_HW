For 10 pages and 2 frames TLB ratio is the same as for 10 pages and 4 frames.

According to the results, I concluded that TLB miss ratio does not depend on the chosen algorithm and number of frames in RAM because TLB just saves the most recently used pages. TLB miss ratio depends on the number of operations (the length of the reference string) and their sequence and also on a number of pages. As the number of operations and pages decreases, TLB miss ratio decreases (from 0.804000 to 0.600000).
